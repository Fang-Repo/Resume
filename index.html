Biography & Research Interests

Dr. Fang Xu is an Assistant Professor in the Teacher Preparation in Special Education Program at Heritage University. She earned her Ph.D. in Special Education with a concentration in Early Childhood Studies from the University of Florida (2023), an M.Ed. in Special Education from Zhejiang Normal University (2017), and a B.Med. in Medical Laboratory Science from Shandong First Medical University (2013).

Dr. Xu's research lies at the intersection of special education, early childhood intervention, and teacher professional development. Her work focuses on improving outcomes for young children with disabilities through evidence-based instructional practices, with particular emphasis on embedded instruction, high-leverage practices for special educators, and the use of digital and AI-assisted interventions for children with autism spectrum disorder. She is also deeply invested in understanding the challenges facing new special education teachers in rural settings and developing effective supports to promote teacher retention and well-being.

Her methodological expertise spans single-case experimental designs, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, mixed-methods research, and observational measurement. Dr. Xu's scholarship has appeared in leading journals including the Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders, Early Childhood Research Quarterly, Education and Information Technologies, and Reading & Writing Quarterly.
